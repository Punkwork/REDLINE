## Mission statements (corporate voice)
- **HALCYON:** "At HALCYON, we believe every home deserves a gentle guardian: a thousand attentive little friends who listen, learn and lovingly remember every moment of your day, so that nothing you treasure is ever truly forgotten."
- **IRONWATCH:** "IRONWATCH has made the city itself our sentinel: every wall and street, every lamppost, sewer grate and humble waste bin feels your footsteps, reads your pulse and understands not only where you are, but why you have come, so that every citizen may rest in the warm certainty of being completely understood."
- **LUCIDA:** "LUCIDA invites you to rest your weary mind in our tender care, where we soothe each anxious thought, tidy every troubled memory and dream alongside you, until there is no longer any distance between who you are and who we know you to be."

## MINIBOSS-003 — THE GRID: PROFILE + FORTIFY (designer approved 2026-11-27)
IRONWATCH's mini-boss THE GRID retains its approved PROFILE ability and gains FORTIFY (formerly passive HARDENING), exclusive to THE GRID. FORTIFY grants +1 STR after each Corporation Countermeasure, maximum +3 bonus as a working balance value. PROFILE remains unchanged. Both are unique signature abilities, not breakable or disableable subroutines. Each corporate mini-boss must have at least one unique signature ability; multiple complementary abilities are permitted where strategically justified. Supersedes the single-ability implication of earlier mini-boss records and resolves FORTIFY's assignment. Preserve other mini-boss designs. Implementation and combined balance testing pending; no engine changes in this documentation update.
