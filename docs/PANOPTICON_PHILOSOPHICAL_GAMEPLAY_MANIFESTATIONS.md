## Cross-cutting constraints

Knowledge, confidence and relevant authority must converge for proposed enforcement; observation alone is not authority. Repeated advice acceptance is not real consent. Preserve genuine benefits, viable refusal and independence, meaningful strategic choice, and multiple possible responses including indifference. No visible psychological phases, meters, classifications, scores or authored explanation. Immediate fictional transactions and consequences must be intelligible. No real-world monitoring, external telemetry, or presentation-to-rules dependency. Check existing core rules, BOON-001, SIGNAL-001 and copy guards before specification. Do not infer motives from choices or absence of attempts.
