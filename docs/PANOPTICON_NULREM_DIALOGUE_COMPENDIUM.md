## Character direction (proposed)

Philosophical expression through character contradiction: NUL/REM may be simultaneously prisoner, guard and architect. His protective obligations, affection, desire for rest, unreliable memories and curiosity can conflict. The Rustler may relinquish the autonomy NUL/REM longs for. These are interpretive lenses, not new canon or a required character arc. Preserve his established personality ROM, lore and SIGNAL-001 reveal guard. Rare introspection should sit among ordinary warmth, humour, encouragement and tactical assistance. Never turn him into an expositor of the experiment.

## Maintenance and safeguards

Append each distinct line with a permanent NR ID, theme, exact draft copy, candidate context, psychological association, optional paired reference and status. Preserve rejected or revised lines with rationale. Pairing may span encounters or corporations; never announce the contradiction. Do not infer player mental states, create psychological scores or expose the experiment. Preserve discovery rather than disclosure, fictional local-only observation, viable independence, and PHIL-004's prohibition on deliberately inducing helplessness or disengagement. Dialogue cannot authorize rules changes; presentation never feeds rules. Any mechanical trigger requires separately approved rules and four-question review. Ordinary nonmechanical dialogue need not confer strategic benefit. All existing copy guards apply. No implementation authorized.
