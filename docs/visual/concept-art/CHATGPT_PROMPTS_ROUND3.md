## 2. Avatar families (AVATAR-001)

**The designer's direction (2026-11-20):** same artistic style for every avatar, but different kinds of avatar, so the faces don't all look alike. One look at an avatar should tell you **which corporation** it's from, **roughly what it does**, and **what kind of ICE** it is (FORTRESS, TUMBLER or VOID). Sentries should be fun to build. *(Sentries are the VOID type: SENTRY was renamed VOID by NAME-001.)*

### 2.1 The three reads

| Read | Carried by | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Corporation** | Palette, materials, one emblem | **HALCYON** (your home): seafoam mint, cream, salmon and brass; household enamel, bakelite, gingham, lace; a brass **sunrise fan** on a cap, collar or chest. **IRONWATCH** (your street): navy enamel and badge gold with porcelain white; civic hardware, peaked caps, chevrons, whistles; a gold **searchlight-fan badge**. **LUCIDA** (your mind): lilac pearl and rose-copper with peach light; soft rounded wellness devices and robed attendants; a glowing **halo-orbit ring** floating above the head. |
| **What it does** | What the character is | Each ICE is a product or a member of staff from that corporation's service: HALCYON's household appliances and home staff, IRONWATCH's civic machines and officers, LUCIDA's wellness devices and attendants. Its job should be obvious from the object (a consent form is a clipboard, a doorbell cam is a doorbell). |
| **ICE type** | Body plan and pose | **FORTRESS — the wall:** wide, heavy, square-shouldered, built like a safe, vault, cabinet or counter; bolted plates; a barrier in front of it (counter, shutters, vault door); arms folded or both hands planted. **TUMBLER — the spinner:** round, with something that visibly turns (a dial face, a drum, a wheel, a rolodex, a carousel); playful, off-balance pose. **VOID — the watcher (sentry):** built around one big lens or sensor; on a stalk, tripod or hover-fins; one "stinger" part that does the harm; leans forward, tracking you. |

**Faces:** no two ICE of the same corporation share a face type (twins excepted). Pick from: screen face, porthole face, single lens, human face, animal face, mask, object with eyes (no face plate), no face at all. The batch-1 and bellhop avatars already use screen (AUTO-RENEW), human (CAPTCHA) and porthole (ACCEPT ALL / REJECT ALL), so the HALCYON ICE below use the others.
