## 2. What an IRONWATCH avatar has to say at a glance

- **Enforcement, not comfort:** these machines are allowed to act. Cheery = a helpful civic appliance; lethal = it hurts you.
- **PULP:** most of them hit. Something on each should look like it can strike (baton, prongs, ram, net, emitter).
- **They react to you:** VOID ICE lean in, track, sniff, aim.
- **Authority hides its face:** visors, lenses, badges and lamps rather than friendly faces (a contrast with HALCYON's smiling staff).

## 3. Draft creature language (for the designer to choose)

- **A. Tin-toy enforcers (Claude's recommendation).** 1950s wind-up tin police robots and civic machines: chunky, riveted, keys in their backs, painted navy and gold, cheerfully officious; in lethal they unfold into riot gear (batons, shields, sirens). Funny at first glance, intimidating up close. Distinct from HALCYON's cartoon staff and LUCIDA's creatures.
- **B. Heavy civic machinery.** Armoured street furniture and vehicles with weight and menace (bulldozer blades, hydraulic rams, tank treads); no comedy.
- **C. Faceless authority.** Mostly visors, lenses and helmets with nothing behind them; the uncanny of a uniform with no person in it.
