# AVATAR-001 — ChatGPT prompts, round 6 (LUCIDA ICE avatars)

**Date:** 2026-11-20 · **Author:** Claude · **For:** the designer, to paste into ChatGPT
**Why:** the designer wants "completely different avatars for ICE that have very little resemblance to human features, although you can still make out their faces", for LUCIDA, each with "the three separate types of trace level images" (the three moods the game shows as the encounter heats up: cheery while FRIENDLY, wary while CRACKING, lethal at MASK OFF).

**Roster (REDLINE_ICE_CORP3):** Opening Node INTAKE (BASELINE SCAN, ONBOARDING); Tier 1 (SLEEP TRACKER, MOOD SCANNER, DREAMCATCHER, FOCUS MODE, NEURAL HANDSHAKE); Tier 2 (MEMORY PALACE, DREAM AD, EMPATHY ENGINE, NEUROLINK, THERAPIST, SYNAPSE BARRIER, RECALL); Central Node (MEMORY BANK, LIMBIC LOOP, ANIMA); escape (WAKE SIGNAL, DÉJÀ VU, AMNESIA, SLEEP PARALYSIS); Black ICE EGO DEATH. 21 ICE: 20 × 3 moods + EGO DEATH (always lethal) = 61 images, in three prompts.

**Design notes:** each follows AVATAR-001 (LUCIDA halo ring; job shown by the object; body plan by type; no two share a face type), with the new rule that LUCIDA's faces are made from machine parts, not human features. File names use the ICE id with accents dropped (DÉJÀ VU → `DEJA_VU_*`).

No reference images needed: each prompt carries the written style guide. If any earlier mascot is to hand, attaching it helps ChatGPT match the line and grain.

```
STYLE GUIDE: Painted 1950s atompunk / Googie advertising illustration, like a mid-century appliance ad or comic-book cover. Clean confident ink outlines, soft airbrushed gradients, gentle painted texture and a light halftone print grain. Not photographic, not 3D-rendered, not anime. Warm light from the upper left with a coloured rim light.

These are ICE: security programs of LUCIDA, a neural-wellness corporation that sells sleep trackers, focus headbands, mood apps and brain implants, and secretly edits your thoughts. LUCIDA's look: lilac pearl enamel, rose-copper, chrome, frosted glass and soft peach light, smooth rounded 1950s forms, and a glowing halo ring floating above every ICE (LUCIDA's emblem).

THE KEY RULE: these are MACHINES and OBJECTS, not people. Almost no resemblance to human features: no skin, no hair, no noses, no ears, no human hands, no human body shape. But each must still have a face you can read instantly, made from its own parts (lenses, dials, lights, slots, screens, reflections, patterns). Every ICE gets a DIFFERENT kind of face.

Body plan by ICE type: FORTRESS = heavy, square, wide and planted, built like a wall, cabinet or vault, a barrier in front. TUMBLER = round, with something that visibly spins or rotates (a drum, a wheel, a dial, a carousel), playful and off-balance. VOID (a sentry) = built around one big sensor or lens, on a stalk, tripod or hovering fins, with one "stinger" part that does the harm, leaning forward to track you.

Each ICE has three moods, one image each, same object, same pose and framing, change only the expression, light and details:
- cheery: the friendly product. Soft peach light, relaxed, welcoming, the halo glowing calmly.
- wary: suspicious. Lights narrowing or flickering, the halo tilting, a hint of violet, something tensing or turning toward you.
- lethal: hostile, mask off. Cold violet and red light, the face's lights glowing red, harsh rim light, the halo spinning or cracked, the stinger or barrier ready.

Rules for every image: PNG, transparent background, 1024 × 1024. The ICE sits on (or hovers just above) a horizontal ledge along the bottom edge of the image, front-on to three-quarters view, in the left two-thirds of the image, the top of the halo about 60 px below the top edge. NO text, letters, numbers or logos anywhere. Exact file name.

Make these ONE AT A TIME, in this order (cheery, then wary, then lethal for each). After each image, wait until I type "next".

1–3. BASELINE_SCAN_cheery / _wary / _lethal.png — VOID. A floating medical scanner disc on two little hover-fins, pearl-white with rose-copper trim. Face: ONE big round sensor lens as its eye and a thin sweeping scan-line of light as its mouth. Stinger: a fine probe needle under the disc.

Face: two round brass dials as eyes and a card slot as a smile, printed on the front card of the drum. Lethal: the drum spins fast and the slot becomes a shredder.

10–12. MOOD_SCANNER_cheery / _wary / _lethal.png — VOID. A mood-ring gemstone on a chrome gooseneck stalk rising from a weighted base. Face: no features at all except reflections inside the big gem: two bright glints as eyes and a curved reflection as a mouth; the gem's colour shifts with its mood. Stinger: a coiled lead with a suction-cup electrode.
13–15. DREAMCATCHER_cheery / _wary / _lethal.png — FORTRESS. A heavy rose-copper dreamcatcher hoop set upright in a square pedestal like a shield wall, brass feathers hanging from it. Face: the knots of its woven web form two eyes and a mouth, lit from behind. Lethal: the web pulls tight like a net.
16–18. FOCUS_MODE_cheery / _wary / _lethal.png — FORTRESS. A solid square tabletop focus device like a giant pair of horse blinkers on a lilac box, padded and riveted. Face: one narrow visor slit across the front with two small dot lights for eyes, nothing else. Lethal: the blinker panels close in like doors.
19–21. NEURAL_HANDSHAKE_cheery / _wary / _lethal.png — TUMBLER. Two interlocking chrome rings turning through each other like a gyroscope on a stand, sparks passing between them. Face: in the centre where the rings cross, a small round screen shows two dot eyes and a wavy line mouth.
```
