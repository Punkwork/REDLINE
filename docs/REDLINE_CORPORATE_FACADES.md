**Version:** 0.1 · **Date:** 2026-11-17 · **Author:** Claude · **Decision ID:** FACADE-001
**Status:** **APPROVED 2026-11-17** by the designer ("Correct. Approved."); recorded as FACADE-001 in `REDLINE_MASTER_DESIGN.md`. Copy and numbers are working values. §6 questions 4–6 answered the same day; HALCYON's new interface copy (§2a) is PROPOSED.
**Builds on:** the Raygun Gothic direction and VISUAL-DECISIONS-2026-11-17 / VISUAL-APPROVALS-002 / RUSTLER-LOOK-001 in `REDLINE_MASTER_DESIGN.md`; The Friendly Lie (`REDLINE_VISUAL_LANGUAGE.md`); the HALCYON Chrome Deco encounter (CHROME-001). ICE rosters: `REDLINE_ICE_CORP1.md` to `CORP3.md`.

**Goal:** give IRONWATCH and LUCIDA the same treatment HALCYON got in CHROME-001, so that each corporation is a polished Raygun Gothic facade that sells a **different degree of surveillance** as a customer benefit, and the player can feel the difference in routine interactions, not only in the fights.

**What doesn't change:** the Rustler layer (hardware cards, zine HUD; RUSTLER-LOOK-001); rule chips and readability rules (Visual Language §2.1, §2.9); the mood ladder and its triggers (§2.6); the Friendly → Cracking → Strike → Mask off states (§2.5). Bloatware keeps the approved oxide-red Whirlwind cartridge for every corporation; only its corporate name and footer change.

## 1. Three degrees of watching

Each corporation goes one step further into the Rustler's life. The step decides what its interfaces **ask for**, what they **remember**, and what they **give back as a reward**.

| | HALCYON (approved) | IRONWATCH | LUCIDA |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Watches** | Your home | Your street, and who you're with | Your mind |
| **Sells** | Comfort: *"We'll take care of it."* | Safety and belonging: *"Your block is safe."* | Peace of mind: *"Breathe in."* |
| **Asks for** | Presence, routines, voice | Location, identity, associates | Sleep, mood, dreams, consent to "gentle adjustments" |
| **Remembers** | When you're home, what you like | Where you went, who you met, what you carried | What you forgot, and what you'd rather not remember |
| **Rewards** | Convenience: the door opens before you reach it | Status: a safety score, a Trusted Resident badge, the fast lane | Calm: a better night, a lighter mood, fewer bad memories |
| **The turn** | The house won't let you leave | The street won't let you pass | Your own mind stops being yours |

**Voice rule (all three):** the corporation never raises its voice before *Mask off*. As the threat grows, the wording gets *more* polite and *more* certain. The horror is the sincerity.

## 2. Shared Raygun Gothic kit

Every facade is built from the same vocabulary, so the three read as one world:

- **Architecture:** symmetrical halls, a central sealed door or focal object (the encounter's "where the data is"), stepped Deco frames, fluted chrome columns, a sunburst or orbit emblem over the door, polished floors that reflect, an open lower half kept clear for the hand and HUD.
- **Atomic Age motifs:** orbit rings, fins, starbursts, rounded pods, glowing globes, radio towers. Each corporation owns one motif (below).
- **Materials:** chrome and one signature enamel per corporation; glass; one warm metal.
- **ICE:** each ICE is a **domestic or civic appliance** from that corporation's product line, built as a chrome-and-enamel shell with a live face (CHROME-001 method: illustrated empty shell, live face parts, live text and buttons).
- **Rule plaques:** enamel service plaques in the corporation's colours (CHROME-001), always opaque, always at least 4.5:1.
- **The reveal:** the hostile background is an **edit of the same hall** at the same perspective. Nothing is added that wasn't there; the decoration is revealed to be the machinery (CHROME-001 step 4). Cracking shows about 20% of it (VISUAL-APPROVALS-002).

## 3. IRONWATCH — "SafeStreets"

**The facade:** a civic security concourse that feels like a grand 1939 transit hall crossed with a precinct house. People are glad to be here: it's clean, bright and orderly, and the officers wave.

**Signature motif:** the **searchlight fan**: a radiating beam sunburst, used over the central door, on badges, in loading animations.

**Atrium (Friendly):** a long symmetrical concourse in navy enamel and polished brass. Fluted gunmetal pylons with brass caps line both sides. Over the central gate, a stepped ziggurat clock tower carries a gold seven-point badge inside a searchlight-fan sunburst. Terrazzo floor with an inlaid brass street map radiating from the gate. Finned porcelain patrol kiosks with round porthole windows, potted topiary in brass planters, a ceiling of glazed coffers with globe lamps. Daylight, calm, a few distant uniformed figures and civilians queueing politely.

**Atrium (Hostile, same view):** the brass street map on the floor lights up and traces **one path: yours**. The decorative grilles between pylons are holding-cell doors. The porthole kiosks are drone hangars, their finned drones unfolding. The searchlights in the sunburst swing down and converge on the viewer. Barrier posts have risen from the terrazzo across the concourse. Red beacon light on brass.

## 4. LUCIDA — "lucida rest"

**The facade:** a luxury wellness retreat in the style of a 1930s observatory-sanatorium. Everything is soft, quiet and slow. You'd sleep well here.

**Signature motif:** the **halo orbit**: concentric electron-orbit rings around a glowing centre, used as the emblem over the door, around sleep pods and as the breathing animation.

**Atrium (Friendly):** a domed solarium in lilac-pearl enamel and rose-copper. A glass dome overhead shows a pale dawn sky. Under the dome, a ring of streamlined sleep pods like chrome-trimmed capsules, each with a softly glowing halo ring; between them, slim copper columns and frosted-glass screens. In the centre, a still reflecting pool; behind it, the sealed door, crowned by the halo-orbit emblem. Floor of pale terrazzo with concentric inlaid rings. Peach dawn light, a few robed figures resting, potted palms.

**Atrium (Hostile, same view):** the halo rings over the pods tilt down and become **cranial scanners**; the pods are restraint cradles with soft straps. The frosted screens clear: they're one-way observation glass with silhouettes behind. The dome's dawn sky resolves into a lattice of lenses. The reflecting pool shows a brain-like web of light. The colour drains to clinical white with a violet glow. Nothing is broken; everything is spotless.

## 5. Art briefs for generation

Follow the HALCYON assets' format so the encounter code can swap them in: **1586 × 992, no text, no UI**, central door at the horizontal centre, vanishing point at about 40% height, the lower 45% kept as open floor (hand and HUD sit there), symmetrical composition, the hostile image an **edit of the friendly one at the identical perspective**. Record the exact prompts, edit prompts, references and model in `godot/assets/friendly_lie/chrome/SOURCES.md` **at the time of generation** (the HALCYON prompts were lost).

- **IRONWATCH friendly:** "Symmetrical Raygun Gothic civic security concourse, 1939 transit hall meets precinct house, navy enamel and polished brass, fluted gunmetal pylons with brass caps, central stepped ziggurat clock tower over a sealed brass gate, gold seven-point badge inside a searchlight-fan sunburst, terrazzo floor with an inlaid brass street map radiating from the gate, finned porcelain patrol kiosks with porthole windows, brass planters with topiary, glazed coffered ceiling with globe lamps, calm daylight, small distant figures queueing politely, immaculate, no text."

- **LUCIDA friendly:** "Symmetrical Raygun Gothic wellness solarium, 1930s observatory sanatorium, lilac pearl enamel and rose-copper, glass dome with pale dawn sky, ring of streamlined chrome-trimmed sleep pods each with a softly glowing halo ring, slim copper columns, frosted glass screens, still reflecting pool before a sealed door crowned with a concentric orbit-ring emblem, pale terrazzo floor with inlaid concentric rings, peach dawn light, robed figures resting, potted palms, serene, immaculate, no text."
