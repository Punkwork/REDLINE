# REDLINE — WORLD & DIALOGUE BIBLE

**Document:** `REDLINE_BIBLE.md`  
**Established:** 2026-11-09  
**Purpose:** Canon of identity, language, culture, narrative tone, and preserved dialogue for the entire REDLINE world.  
**Relationship to other documents:** The Master Design governs game systems and mechanics; this Bible governs the world's identity and expression. The Current Status tracks outstanding decisions; the Development Log tracks changes. If narrative and mechanical descriptions conflict, flag the discrepancy rather than silently rewriting either document.

> **Guiding principle:** **The Bible defines what REDLINE is and what REDLINE isn't. Its verses reveal how that world lives.**

NUL/REM is one voice through which REDLINE becomes real, but not the world itself. A Runner's slang, a manufacturer's obsolete designation, the sound of a CAP being opened, a dealer's evasive joke, the threat of VOID, and the rules of a corporate network all carry the same responsibility: they must make the setting feel as though it existed before the player arrived.

## 1. The Bible's authority and scope

This is a **world bible**, not merely a NUL/REM dialogue bible. It preserves:

- **Identity:** REDLINE's defining premises, tone, themes, and contradictions.
- **Boundaries:** what would feel false or out of place in this world.
- **Language:** corporate nomenclature, Runner slang, terminology drift, euphemisms, and the gap between official claims and lived reality.
- **Culture and material history:** how objects are made, handled, bought, repurposed, distrusted, and talked about.
- **Voices:** NUL/REM, Runners, dealers, corporations, ICE/system messaging, and voices not yet invented.
- **Verses:** exact, preserved lines of dialogue, sayings, slogans, and fragments that demonstrate the world's identity.
- **Delivery:** suggested contexts, narrative purposes, and implementation hooks, without treating suggestions as locked mechanics.

### Canon status labels

- **CANON:** User-approved world fact, name, or preserved line. Preserve verbatim where quoted.
- **DIRECTION:** Established aesthetic or narrative principle; details may evolve.
- **CANDIDATE:** Promising idea not yet approved; never present as established fact.
- **RETIRED:** Superseded but retained as design history, not for current use.
- **OPEN:** A decision still to be made.

**Preservation rule:** Never silently alter, overwrite, or delete a CANON verse. A revision receives a new identifier or version with a note explaining its relationship to the original. Do not mistake a proposed trigger, a character's lie, or corporate propaganda for objective world truth.

## 2. What REDLINE is

**DIRECTION —** A hostile, layered cyberpunk world in which Runners infiltrate corporate networks; human bodies, memory, consciousness, and code are entangled with real consequences. Technology has institutional history and street-level afterlives. Its language is shaped by corporate standardization, black markets, obsolescence, and the practical shorthand of people who risk their lives using it.

**DIRECTION —** Retro-computing sensibility and aggressive cyberpunk/arcade energy coexist. Corporate bureaucracy can be absurd; danger is not. Humor should deepen credibility, not erase stakes.

**CANON —** The First Signal is an actual synchronization transmission. REDLINE explores the human/machine boundary and the possibility of crossing it. The larger mystery should not be prematurely explained through incidental dialogue.

**CANON —** VOID is not a routine injury: it signifies deletion of portions of consciousness. Its established thematic expression is: **“Death ends a Runner. VOID leaves someone behind.”**

### What REDLINE is not

- Not a world where every invention exists solely to explain a gameplay mechanic.
- Not a generic collection of neon cyberpunk words without etymology, physicality, or social context.
- Not a comedy in which constant quips neutralize fear, loss, or moral weight.
- Not a setting in which official corporate language is automatically trustworthy.
- Not a setting where NUL/REM is the sole narrator or source of authenticity.
- Not a world whose mysteries must be explained immediately or completely.

These are **editorial boundaries (DIRECTION)**, not additional combat rules.

## 3. The world's authenticity test

Before canonizing a new name, device, institution, practice, or line, ask:

1. **Origin:** Who invented or formalized it, and why?
2. **Evolution:** What do people call it now, and why did the language change?
3. **Material reality:** What does a person physically do with it, hear, see, feel, or risk?
4. **Social meaning:** Who trusts it, profits from it, fears it, or jokes about it?
5. **Narrative use:** What does this detail reveal without an exposition dump?
6. **Fit:** Does it reinforce the world's established themes and constraints?

Not every detail needs all six answers immediately. The test is a tool for consistency, not an excuse to invent unnecessary lore.

## 4. Language, artifacts, and lived culture

### CAPS — foundational case study (CANON)

- **Contemporary name:** **CAP**; plural **CAPS**.
- **Historical corporate classification:** **Disposable Autonomous Cartridge (DAC)**.
- **Category:** A single-use tactical injection device, separate from cards, Hardware, and Ghostware.
- **Physical ritual:** **Flip. Inject. Discard.** A CAP contains one-time-use injectable code; its physical form and the digital act of injection reinforce one another.
- **Language drift:** Corporate DAC became colloquial CAP. Runners speak of **burning** a CAP; the original formal classification survives as history rather than everyday speech.
- **Cultural contrast:** Corporate certification and underground imitation invite skepticism, black-market humor, and unreliable assurances.

**CANON mechanical cross-reference:** JACK OUT is a distinct CAP, not a universal free action. Its use consumes the CAP, costs no BW, cannot function under LOCKDOWN, retreats to the preceding junction, fully resets the abandoned node, grants no node-completion rewards, reduces TRACE by an amount not yet specified, and does not heal Vitae or remove VOID. Precise activation windows, inventory limits, and acquisition are **OPEN**. Detailed rules belong in the Master Design.

**DIRECTION:** CAPS demonstrate how an ordinary gameplay category can carry a history, a tactile ritual, an economy, a vocabulary, and a sense of place. Future concepts should aspire to this degree of coherence without copying the same origin story.

### Other canonical language anchors

- **BURN:** Vitae loss; flesh harmed by hostile ICE.
- **SYN:** Runner-selected hand discard; each unfulfilled point causes 2 Vitae damage after available cards are discarded (RULE-126).
- **VOID:** Persistent loss of consciousness expressed as reduced DRAW; removal requires a specialized recovery node.
- **ICE / DEADLOCK / TRACE / LOCKDOWN:** Functional network vocabulary with consequential gameplay meanings. The fiction should respect those meanings rather than use them as interchangeable decoration.

## 5. Voices of REDLINE

### NUL/REM — one facet of the world

**DIRECTION:** A sardonic, morose, historically minded AI companion, often dryly protective of the Runner. Its care is more credible when concealed within practical criticism. It can be mistaken, evasive, or an unreliable witness to its own history. Its voice should carry experience, not simply deliver tooltips.

**Six established modes:** The Pedant; The Cynic; The Melancholic; The Reluctant Guardian; The Unreliable Historian; The Occasionally Delighted.

**Voice boundary:** NUL/REM is not a perpetual joke dispenser, omniscient lore encyclopedia, or transparent emotional confidant. Especially around VOID, restraint or silence may communicate more than humor.

### Other voices

- **Runners:** Pragmatic, compressed, risk-aware slang; their speech reflects lived cost.
- **Dealers and underground operators:** Salesmanship, dark humor, half-truths, local expertise.
- **Corporations:** Standards, classifications, assurances, disclaimers, and branding; these may conceal consequences.
- **Network/ICE systems:** Operational vocabulary and threat signaling, distinct from conversational personalities.

These are **DIRECTION**, not rigid dialect prescriptions. More voices may be defined as the world grows.

## 6. The Verses — immutable seed archive

**Verse IDs** identify preserved source text. A verse can be a spoken exchange, slogan, aphorism, or piece of cultural language. A verse's **speaker** and **function** must be recorded independently: not every great REDLINE line belongs to NUL/REM. Quoted wording is preserved; triggers and placement are suggestions unless explicitly approved.

### VERSE WL-001 — Flip it. Inject it. Forget it.
**Status:** CANON · **Voice:** Historical corporate slogan / ironic Runner saying · **Function:** Industrial history, physical ritual, linguistic survival.

> “Flip it. Inject it. Forget it.”

**Possible placement (OPEN):** Old packaging, a vendor sign, item lore, or a Runner repeating the slogan ironically.

**Status:** CANON · **Voice:** World aphorism, speaker unassigned · **Function:** Stakes, existential horror, moral identity.

## 7. How to add future verses

Use the following template; preserve the spoken text exactly once approved:

```markdown
### VERSE [PREFIX]-[NUMBER] — [Short title]
**Status:** CANDIDATE / CANON / RETIRED
**Voices:** [Speakers or cultural source]
**Function:** [What the line reveals about the world]
**Context / trigger:** [Suggested; label OPEN until approved]
**Exact text:**
> [Verbatim dialogue or saying]
**Notes:** [Origin, variants, contradictions, usage limits]
```

**Suggested prefixes:** `NR` = NUL/REM; `WL` = world language/lore; future prefixes can distinguish other voices. Prefixes organize the archive, not a hierarchy of importance.

### Editorial workflow

1. Capture candidate lines, including spontaneous dialogue and offhand phrases.
2. Identify speaker, situation, and what the line reveals about the setting.
3. Mark **CANDIDATE** until approved; never retroactively imply approval.
4. On approval, assign a permanent verse ID and preserve exact wording.
5. Record variants separately; never overwrite canon text.
6. When implementing, link gameplay dialogue assets back to verse IDs and track triggers, frequency, and repetition separately.

## 8. Open worldbuilding questions

- How old is the DAC classification, and what corporate entities originally standardized it?
- What is the precise physical interface used to inject a CAP?
- What forms of CAP counterfeiting, recycling, and certification are common?
- How do different Runner communities speak differently?
- Which fragments of NUL/REM's remembered history are genuine, fabricated, or impossible?
- How does the world talk about VOID when it is trying not to admit what VOID really is?

**OPEN means open.** None of these questions should be answered silently in implementation or supporting copy.

## Cosmological guidepost — LOCKED (2026-11-09)

### Every consciousness has a first signal.

> Throughout existence, awareness has emerged, and intelligences have reached beyond themselves to communicate.
>
> These events may have occurred millions of times across the eons.
>
> The First Signal is not necessarily one historical transmission, but a recurring threshold: the moment awareness begins, or one consciousness first reaches another.
>
> REDLINE explores what happens when those thresholds are crossed, and what may emerge on the other side.

**Scope:** This is a CANON worldbuilding guidepost, not a finalized technical explanation of the First Signal. The existing description of a literal synchronization transmission remains part of the storyline; its relationship to the recurring threshold of awareness and communication is still to be discovered. Do not assert a single cosmic origin, deterministic cycle, or definitive metaphysical explanation without further design approval.

**Narrative application:** The Runner/NUL/REM relationship, human/AI tension, corporate control, neural signaling, and discoveries across the network can reflect this guidepost without announcing its meaning at the outset. Distinguish actual transmissions and observable signals from philosophical interpretations of consciousness.

## PANOPTICON — surveillance, confinement, and the transformation of humanity (2026-11-10)

**Status: DIRECTION — foundational thematic pillar.** **Title status: working title PANOPTICON;** REDLINE remains the existing repository/document codename pending deliberate renaming and title clearance.

### The governing question

> **What does humanity become when it knows it is being permanently observed and monitored?**

This is the central question, not merely whether a Runner can evade cameras, escape a prison, or discover an observer. PANOPTICON examines what permanent observation does to human consciousness, identity, behaviour, culture, creativity, intimacy, and the very idea of a private self. The question must remain genuinely open: the setting should not presuppose that humanity simply becomes less human, nor prescribe one uniform transformation.

**DIRECTION — The internalized observer.** Surveillance need not always be actively enforced to change behaviour. People may anticipate scrutiny, adapt their actions, and eventually experience observation as normal, reassuring, necessary, or constitutive of identity. The possibility of observation may exert power even when no observer is visible.

**DIRECTION — Generational transformation.** Explore a society whose inhabitants have lived with pervasive observation for so long that privacy may seem unfamiliar, suspect, or frightening. Different individuals and communities may respond differently: accommodation, performance, creativity, resistance, dependence, withdrawal, and unexpected new forms of social life. Avoid reducing everyone to frightened contemporary people transplanted into a dystopia.

**DIRECTION — Control is more than detection.** Surveillance can classify, predict, condition, and constrain behaviour. The thematic focus is surveillance as a system of power, not merely an inventory of monitoring devices.

**DIRECTION — The paradox of intrusion and imprisonment.** The Runner voluntarily penetrates a network designed to deny access, then faces systems designed to contain and observe them. The tension between being **locked out** and **locked in** should recur in setting, narrative, interface, and sound.

### Architectural and visual language

**DIRECTION — Lock motif.** Locks, deadbolts, sealed thresholds, vaults, airlocks, containment mechanisms, apertures, and claustrophobic spaces express restricted access and control. The core encounter rule — breaking ICE **DEADLOCK** subroutines to permit advancement after the corporation turn — is an especially important opportunity to give the game's central action a visual and auditory identity. Literal doors are optional, not mandatory.

**DIRECTION — Panoptic network.** Inspired by the panopticon's asymmetry of visibility: a central or concealed observer can potentially watch the confined while the confined cannot know whether observation is occurring. Corporate network spaces may evoke surveillance prisons, concentric structures, opaque observation windows, impossible corridors, and watchful architecture, but need not literally reproduce a circular prison. Psychological pressure matters more than literal architectural fidelity.

**DIRECTION — Mechanics should express theme.** Supporting corporate defences may observe Runner behaviour and react to patterns. The player may begin anticipating surveillance and voluntarily changing tactics. This should emerge from legible, fair, strategically interesting rules; never use the theme to excuse arbitrary punishment. No separate Observation resource is approved.

**DIRECTION — The deck as confined space.** Corporate corruption can occupy draws and hand slots, limiting the Runner's available actions. This is a thematic connection, not an authorization to implement unapproved corruption cards or change established damage rules.

### Narrative boundaries and open questions

- **OPEN:** What transformations arise under permanent observation, and do they differ across communities or generations?
- **OPEN:** What does it mean to be unobserved in this society? Could solitude itself become frightening?
- **OPEN:** Is there a central observer, a distributed system, or something else? Do not decide this prematurely.
- **OPEN:** How does NUL/REM's attentive, sometimes protective presence contrast with institutional observation?
- **OPEN:** How, if at all, does this theme intersect with the First Signal and consciousness? The existing cosmological guidepost and actual synchronization transmission remain intact.
- **OPEN:** Final title availability, typography, environmental art, surveillance gameplay details, and narrative resolution.

**Editorial test:** Every major thematic use of surveillance should ask what observation *changes in people*, not merely what technology sees. Preserve ambiguity and human variation rather than converting the game into a predetermined lecture.


## RULE-126 — SYN damage terminology and overflow (LOCKED, 2026-11-11)

**Authoritative decision:** The active damage keyword is **SYN X** (also styled **SYN DAMAGE X** in the approved rule review). Legacy **NEURAL DAMAGE** and **SYNAPTIC DAMAGE** are deprecated; neither is an active separate damage type. SYN is **Runner-chosen** hand discard, never random discard. Historical mentions of the old terms elsewhere are superseded, not additional active mechanics.

Resolve SYN X in this order:
1. The Runner chooses and discards as many cards as possible from their current hand, up to X.
2. Each unfulfilled point of SYN causes **2 Vitae damage**.
3. Apply that Vitae damage **after** discarding the available cards.

| SYN X | Hand | Discarded | Vitae damage |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 3 | 3 | 3 | 0 |
| 3 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| 3 | 1 | 1 | 4 |
| 3 | 0 | 0 | 6 |
| 5 | 2 | 2 | 6 |

**Priority:** RULE-126 supersedes older no-Vitae-loss statements for hand-discard damage and older random-discard SYN definitions. BURN remains direct Vitae damage; VOID remains persistent draw reduction. The approved overflow outcome is Vitae damage; do not silently reinterpret it as a separate SYN discard or VOID effect. Other prevention and trigger sequencing rules remain subject to their explicit text; do not invent unresolved interactions.

**Source:** `REDLINE_RULE_REVIEW_126.md`, approved and locked 2026-11-11. **Regression:** verify all five table cases and discard-before-Vitae ordering. RULE-127 remains pending.


## Vitae / VIT — Runner health terminology (LOCKED, 2026-11-11)

**Vitae** is the canonical name of the Runner's health resource, abbreviated **VIT** in compact UI, cards, and status displays. The former term is deprecated. **BURN damage** reduces Vitae; SYN and VOID retain their separate mechanics, except for any explicitly defined BURN overflow.


**Damage terminology lock (2026-11-11):** **CHAR** is deprecated and replaced by **BURN damage**. **BURN X reduces Runner Vitae by X**, subject to applicable BURN prevention. Earlier CHAR labels, examples, and prevention-pool references in this document are to be read as BURN. SYN remains mandatory Runner-selected discard with its approved overflow rule; VOID remains distinct. This is a terminology replacement, not an additional damage type.
